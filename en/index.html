About

A developer who understands calmly and follows through

I tend not to jump to an answer too quickly. I first try to understand the user's situation and workflow, then break the problem into smaller pieces that can be built, reviewed, and improved. When I meet an unfamiliar domain or technology, I focus on organizing the context and turning it into something practical.

In my work, I try to leave clear requirements, constraints, risks, and handoff notes so that teammates can continue from where I stopped. My working style is calm and persistent: when something breaks, I prefer tracing logs, configuration, and data flow to find the cause rather than only patching the visible symptom.
